## Biography (English)

Yukako Tohsato received her M.E from Kyushu Institute of Technology University, Japan, in 1997. After working at Mitsubishi Electric Co., she received her Ph.D. degree from Osaka University in 2002. After working at Osaka University and Ritsumeikan University, she was a research scientist at RIKEN Quantitative Biology Center from 2013 to 2017 and an associate professor at Osaka Electro-Communication University from 2017 to 2019. She is currently a professor at Ritsumeikan University. Her research interest is in computational biology, bioinformatics, and bioimage informatics. Engage in research related to the analysis of omics data, including phenome, with a focus on metabolism.
